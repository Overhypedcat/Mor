Mor needs its helper on this PC

Mor's extension does its work through a small helper program that runs on your PC while you watch Twitch. It isn't installed yet. It's for Windows 10 and 11.

  1. Download Mor from its latest release on GitHub: Source code (zip).
  2. Unzip it, and double-click Install Mor.cmd in the folder.
  3. When it says Mor is set up, come back here and press Check again.

    How to add Proton VPN servers
    1. Make a free account at protonvpn.com, or sign in to yours.
    2. Open account.protonvpn.com/downloads and scroll to WireGuard configuration.
    3. Give the config any name, and pick Windows as the platform.
    4. Under VPN options, set NetShield blocker filtering to No filter.
    5. In the server list, pick a free server in the Netherlands or the United States and press Create.
    6. Press Download: you get a .conf file. Make two or three, on different servers, in case one is busy.
    7. Here, press Add servers and choose the .conf files.

    The free plan allows one connection at a time: while Mor uses a server, keep the Proton app disconnected.
